How combos work in Clank!: a combo card reads from the board (have a crown? have a companion?) or from your play state (made 6 skill? trashed a card?). Cards that provide a resource (a Companion type, a freed prisoner, a Burgle to trash) enable other cards to trigger. The clusters below group cards by combo theme.

Combo participation overview

Goal: in the final 100-card deck, maximize the % of cards that participate in 2+ combo clusters while still hitting the type/ratio/dragon targets. Below: distribution (how many cards have N cluster memberships), cluster health (each cluster needs providers AND triggers to fire), and orphans (cards in 0 clusters — combo deadweight, avoid unless they bring raw stats you need).

Combo orphans (0 clusters)

These cards don't combo with anything. They can still be useful for raw stats (e.g., a 3-skill card buys you a 3-cost card), but if you have a choice, prefer a card that participates in clusters.

Trash policy breakdown

Your preference: active trash (trash a card from your deck/discard/play) is preferred over self-trash (cards that trash themselves). The Underworld will add Trash-payoff cards — cards that reward you when something else trashes them. The current pool has very little active trash; this should expand significantly with Lairs and Underworld.
